Understanding Pokies RTP and Volatility

To maximize your chances of winning at Australian online pokies, you must understand two critical mathematical concepts:

  • RTP (Return to Player): This is the theoretical percentage of wagered money a game pays back over millions of spins. We recommend sticking to games with an RTP of 96.00% or higher to ensure long-term value.
  • Volatility (Variance): Low volatility games pay out smaller amounts frequently, making them great for completing bonus wagering requirements. High volatility games, which are currently the most popular in Australia, have longer dry spells but offer the potential for massive, life-changing payouts in a single spin.
